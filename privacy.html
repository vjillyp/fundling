Fundling Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Fundling is developed by Vanessa J. Panaligan. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Fundling iOS app.

Information You Enter

Holdings, balances, institution names, notes, savings goals, balance history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. These entries are not transmitted to the developer or Thunkable. Fundling does not require an account or connect to your bank accounts.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Fundling does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or automatic crash-reporting services that collect end-user data. Thunkable has confirmed that its runtime does not collect or transmit end-user data.

Optional App Lock

The optional app lock uses your device's authentication features. Fundling does not receive or store your Face ID facial data. App lock controls access to the app; it is not a claim that stored entries are encrypted.

Storage and Deletion

Fundling provides no account, cloud sync, or in-app cloud backup. You can edit or delete holdings and goals within the app. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase your locally stored records. Any backups managed by your device or operating system are governed by its settings and provider's policies.

Because the developer does not receive your ledger entries, the developer cannot retrieve or restore them for you.

External Links

Educational links open third-party websites in your browser. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information when you visit them. Fundling does not send your ledger entries to these websites.

Support Emails

If you email fundling.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues, not for advertising. Email is processed through Gmail under Google's policies.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the same address. Please do not send passwords, account numbers, or sensitive financial records.

This Website

This support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its own privacy statement. This is separate from information stored in the Fundling app.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's practices change. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact fundling.support@gmail.com.

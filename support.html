Fundling Support

Track your assets and goals with a simple manual ledger.

Need help or want to share feedback?

Email fundling.support@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, account numbers, or other sensitive financial information.

How do I update my balances?

Choose Update Balances from Overview or Holdings, enter the new amounts, and save your changes. Fundling does not connect to banks or update balances automatically.

Why does my balance history change?

History reflects recorded asset totals. Changes may include deposits, withdrawals, or holdings added or removed. The graph does not measure investment returns.

Where is my information stored?

Your holdings are stored locally on your device. Fundling has no account, cloud sync, or in-app cloud backup. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase your records.

How can I hide my amounts?

Use the visibility button on Overview or the Hide Amounts switch in Settings.

Which currency is supported?

Fundling currently supports U.S. dollars (USD) only.
